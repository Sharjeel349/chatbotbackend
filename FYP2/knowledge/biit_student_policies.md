# BIIT Student Policies and Guidelines

## Admission Policy and Eligibility Criteria
- **Programs Covered**: BSCS, BETI, and BET Cyber Security (Fall 2026 onwards).
- **Academic Eligibility**: Minimum 50% marks in HSSC / A-Levels (12 years of schooling) or IBCC-equivalent qualification.
- **Intermediate Criteria**: Minimum 50% marks in Intermediate Part-I in Pre-Engineering, Pre-Medical, ICS, DAE, or an IBCC-recognized equivalent.
- **Failures**: Any candidate with a failure in any subject at the Intermediate level is strictly not eligible for admission.
- **Non-Mathematics Students**: Candidates admitted without Mathematics at Intermediate level must pass Mathematics deficiency courses during Year 1.
- **Provisional Status**: Admission remains provisional until Intermediate Part-II is passed and original result cards/certificates are verified.
- **Document Submission**: Complete hard copies of admission forms, required documents, attested photocopies, signed affidavit, and fee deposit challan must reach the Admission Office at least one day before merit list display.
- **Disqualification**: Forged documents or false information result in immediate admission cancellation and disciplinary action. Missing notified deadlines results in automatic cancellation.

## Shift Assignment and Shift Change Policy
- **Shifts**: Programs operate in Morning and Afternoon shifts.
- **Shift Rule**: Once a student is assigned to either Morning or Afternoon shift, they must remain in that shift for the entire duration of the degree program.
- **Shift Change Prohibition**: No shift change is allowed under any circumstances after assignment.

## Semester Enrollment and Credit Hour Limits
- **Enrollment Requirement**: Students must enroll at the start of every semester via the official enrollment form and fee clearance.
- **Credit Hour Load**: Minimum 15 credit hours and maximum 21 credit hours per semester (in accordance with HEC undergraduate policy).
- **Exam Eligibility**: Exam eligibility is blocked until all financial dues and fees are cleared.
- **Late Enrollment**: Late enrollment with late fee may be permitted up to 7 days after semester commencement, subject to departmental approval.
- **Unapproved Absence**: Skipping a semester without prior official approval results in cancellation of admission.
- **CMS Registration**: Departments must cancel a ceased student's registration on CMS immediately upon result declaration.

## Fee Payment and Installment Options
- **Installment Facility**: Semester tuition fee may be paid in 2, 3, or 4 installments at the student's choice.
- **Dues Clearance**: All fee installments must be cleared before exam datesheets and admit slips are issued.

## Degree Duration and Maximum Semesters
- **Standard Program Duration**: Standard undergraduate program length is 8 semesters (4 years).
- **Maximum Permissible Semesters**: Students are allowed up to a maximum limit of 12 registered semesters to complete all degree requirements.
- **Drop Rule**: Any student who has not completed all degree requirements by the end of their 12th semester is permanently dropped from the program.

## Promotion, Academic Cease, and Semester Repeat Policy
- **Promotion Requirement**: Promotion to the next semester requires meeting the minimum required cumulative CGPA and academic requirements.
- **Academic Cease**: Falling below the required cumulative CGPA causes the student to be ceased from university rolls.
- **Residency Impact**: Ceased duration does not count toward the student's academic residency period.
- **Repeating a Semester**: A student who fails to meet promotion requirements is allowed to repeat the entire semester once during the entire degree program. New grades replace old grades.
- **Second Cease (Permanent Drop)**: A second cease is permanent. The student is permanently dismissed with no further enrollment permitted in any university program.

## Minimum Cumulative CGPA Progression Requirements
- **CGPA Progression Rule**: The minimum required cumulative CGPA increases by 0.25 each semester, reaching 2.50 at semester 8 for graduation:
  - **Semester 2**: Minimum cumulative CGPA required is **1.00**
  - **Semester 3**: Minimum cumulative CGPA required is **1.25**
  - **Semester 4**: Minimum cumulative CGPA required is **1.50**
  - **Semester 5**: Minimum cumulative CGPA required is **1.75**
  - **Semester 6**: Minimum cumulative CGPA required is **2.00**
  - **Semester 7**: Minimum cumulative CGPA required is **2.25**
  - **Semester 8 (Graduation)**: Minimum cumulative CGPA required is **2.50**
- **Cease Condition**: A student whose cumulative CGPA falls below the required threshold for their respective semester is ceased from university rolls.

## Attendance Policy and Leave Rules
- **Attendance Mandate**: Minimum **75% attendance** is strictly mandatory in every registered course.
- **Leave Allowance**: Up to 25% absence/leave is allowed solely for genuine emergencies, documented sickness, or illness.
- **Absolute Ceiling**: Course attendance must never fall below 75%, regardless of the reason.
- **Examination Bar**: Students failing to achieve 75% attendance are barred from sitting in mid-term or final-term examinations for that course.

## Examination Rules, Conduct, and Cheating Penalties
- **Arrival Time**: Candidates must arrive at the examination center at least 15 minutes before the scheduled exam start time.
- **Assigned Seating**: Candidates must sit strictly at their assigned seat according to the official seating plan.
- **Student ID Display**: The official Student ID card must be displayed visibly on the desk throughout the examination.
- **Prohibited Items**: Mobile phones, smart watches, digital gadgets, and unauthorized electronic devices are strictly prohibited inside the exam hall.
- **Material Sharing**: Exchanging calculators, pens, stationery, or study materials during the exam is forbidden.
- **Cheating Penalty**: Any cheating, possession of unauthorized materials, or unfair means is reported immediately to the administration. Penalty includes exam disqualification for up to **3 years**.

## Academic Conduct and Outcome-Based Education (OBE)
- **OBE Curriculum**: Programs follow Higher Education Commission (HEC) guidelines and an Outcome-Based Education (OBE) system tailored to software industry standards.
- **Holistic Competencies**: Emphasis is placed on technical skills, soft skills, professionalism, ethics, and teamwork.
- **Academic Calendar**: Mid-Term and Final-Term examinations strictly follow the official University Academic Calendar.
- **Assignments and Projects**: Timely submission of term projects, quizzes, and assignments under teacher guidance is mandatory.
